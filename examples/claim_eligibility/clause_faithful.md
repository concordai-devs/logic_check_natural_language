## 4. Claims

**4.1 Conditions of cover.** The Insurer shall have no liability in respect of
any claim where (a) the Policy was not in force on the date of loss, whether by
reason of non-payment of premium, lapse, or cancellation, or (b) the loss was
proximately caused by an Excluded Peril.

**4.2 Deductible.** Where the assessed loss does not exceed the Deductible, the
claim shall be admitted but no payment shall be due under the Policy.

**4.3 Payment.** Subject to Sections 4.1 and 4.2, the Insurer shall pay the
assessed loss less the Deductible (the "**Claim Amount**") as follows:

  (a) the full Claim Amount, where the Insured has given notice of the claim
      within thirty (30) days of the date of loss and has provided complete
      supporting documentation in accordance with Section 4.4; or

  (b) the Claim Amount reduced by fifteen per cent (15%), where the Insured
      has failed to give notice within thirty (30) days of the date of loss,
      or has given timely notice but has not provided complete supporting
      documentation.

**4.4 Documentation.** Complete supporting documentation means proof of loss,
proof of ownership, and evidence of valuation, each in a form reasonably
acceptable to the Insurer.

**4.5 Definitions.** "**Excluded Peril**" means flood, war, or a wilful act of
the Insured; "**Deductible**" means the amount specified in the Schedule.
