## 7. Severance

**7.1 Entitlement.** On termination of the Employee's employment, the
Employee's sole and exclusive entitlement to severance shall be as set out in
this Section 7.

**7.2 Qualifying service.** The Employee shall not be entitled to any
severance payment unless, as at the Termination Date, the Employee has
completed twelve (12) months of continuous service with the Employer.

**7.3 Dismissal for Cause.** Subject to Section 7.2, where the Employer
terminates the employment for Cause, no severance payment shall be due, save
that where the Cause relied upon is not gross misconduct and the Employee has
completed five (5) years of continuous service, the Employee shall receive the
minimum severance payment, if any, required by applicable law.

**7.4 Resignation.** No severance payment shall be due on the Employee's
resignation, except that a resignation in direct response to a fundamental
breach of this Agreement by the Employer (a "**Constructive Dismissal**")
shall be treated as a termination by the Employer without Cause, with the
Employee's entitlement determined under Sections 7.5 and 7.6.

**7.5 Redundancy.** Subject to Sections 7.2 to 7.4, where the employment is
terminated by reason of redundancy the Employee shall receive:

  (a) the Standard Severance Payment, where suitable alternative employment
      has been offered by the Employer;

  (b) the Premium Redundancy Payment, where no suitable alternative
      employment has been offered and the Employee has completed five (5)
      years of continuous service; or

  (c) the Enhanced Severance Payment, in any other case.

**7.6 Other terminations.** Subject to Sections 7.2 to 7.4, in any case not
falling within Section 7.5 the Employee shall receive:

  (a) where the Employer has given the Employee written notice of
      termination: the Enhanced Severance Payment where the Employee has
      completed five (5) years of continuous service, and the Standard
      Severance Payment otherwise; or

  (b) the Enhanced Severance Payment, where no written notice of termination
      has been given by the Employer.

**7.7 Definitions.** In this Section 7: "**Cause**" means gross misconduct,
persistent underperformance following written warning, or a material breach of
the Employee's duties; "**Standard Severance Payment**" means an amount equal
to one (1) month's base salary for each completed year of service;
"**Enhanced Severance Payment**" means the Standard Severance Payment plus an
amount equal to three (3) months' base salary; and "**Premium Redundancy
Payment**" means the Standard Severance Payment plus an amount equal to six
(6) months' base salary.
