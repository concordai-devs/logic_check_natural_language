## 7. Severance

**7.1 Entitlement.** Upon termination of the Employee's employment, the
Employee shall be entitled to a severance payment determined in accordance
with this Section 7, which sets out the Employee's sole and exclusive
entitlement to severance.

**7.2 Dismissal for Cause.** Where the employment is terminated by the
Employer for Cause:

  (a) no severance payment shall be due where the Cause relied upon consists
      of gross misconduct; and

  (b) in any other case of dismissal for Cause, no severance payment shall be
      due unless, as at the Termination Date, the Employee has completed five
      (5) years of continuous service, in which case the Employee's
      entitlement shall be limited to the minimum severance payment, if any,
      required by applicable law.

**7.3 Resignation.** No severance payment shall be due where the employment
ends by reason of the Employee's resignation; provided that a resignation by
the Employee in direct response to a fundamental breach of this Agreement by
the Employer (a "**Constructive Dismissal**") shall not be treated as a
resignation for the purposes of this Section 7, and the Employee's entitlement
shall instead be determined under Sections 7.4 to 7.6 as if the employment had
been terminated by the Employer without Cause.

**7.4 Qualifying service.** Where the Employee's entitlement falls to be
determined under this Section 7.4 to 7.6 and, as at the Termination Date, the
Employee has not completed twelve (12) months of continuous service, the
Employee's entitlement shall be limited to the minimum severance payment, if
any, required by applicable law.

**7.5 Redundancy.** Where the Employee has completed twelve (12) months of
continuous service and the employment is terminated by reason of redundancy:

  (a) where the Employer has offered the Employee suitable alternative
      employment, the Employee shall receive the Standard Severance Payment;

  (b) where no suitable alternative employment has been offered and the
      Employee has completed five (5) years of continuous service, the
      Employee shall receive the Premium Redundancy Payment; and

  (c) in any other case, the Employee shall receive the Enhanced Severance
      Payment.

**7.6 Other terminations.** In any other case in which the Employee has
completed twelve (12) months of continuous service, the Employee shall
receive:

  (a) where the Employer has given notice of termination in writing: the
      Enhanced Severance Payment, irrespective of length of service; or

  (b) where the Employer has failed to give notice of termination in writing:
      the Enhanced Severance Payment if the Employee has completed five (5)
      years of continuous service, and otherwise the Standard Severance
      Payment.

**7.7 Definitions.** In this Section 7: "**Cause**" means gross misconduct,
persistent underperformance following written warning, or a material breach of
the Employee's duties; "**Standard Severance Payment**" means an amount equal
to one (1) month's base salary for each completed year of service;
"**Enhanced Severance Payment**" means the Standard Severance Payment plus an
amount equal to three (3) months' base salary; and "**Premium Redundancy
Payment**" means the Standard Severance Payment plus an amount equal to six
(6) months' base salary.
